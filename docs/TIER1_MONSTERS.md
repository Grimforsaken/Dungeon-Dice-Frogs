# Tier 1 Monster Variants

Tier 1 covers dungeon floors 1–10 and uses two variants of each of the seven Feed the Frog bugs.

## Art identity corrections

These visual identities are fixed and should not be relabeled:

- **Fly** — black fuzzy body, very large red eyes, damaged/translucent wings.
- **Mosquito** — long proboscis, red segmented abdomen, thin insect body.
- **Dragonfly** — blue/gold dragon-like insect with four large wings and a long tail.
- **Butterfly** — blue/gold/yellow ornate butterfly-like insect.
- **Bee** — yellow/black bee-like humanoid insect; one variant carries a shield.
- **Poison Fly** — green/purple toxic insect with visible poison/slime effects.
- **Firefly** — orange/red fire insect using actual flames.

The black fuzzy red-eyed Fly must **not** be labeled Mosquito or Dragonfly.

## Weapon behavior

Monster attacks use the same physical weapon behavior as player weapons unless a later monster-specific rule overrides it.

- **Dagger** — d2 physical damage; 2 attacks; DEX extra-attack check can produce a 3rd strike.
- **Short Sword** — d4 physical damage; 1 attack; DEX extra-attack check can produce a 2nd strike; 15% bleed chance per successful attack.
- **Axe** — d6 physical damage; 1 attack; 30% bleed chance per successful attack.
- **Blunt** — d8 physical damage; 1 attack; ignores shields and 2 AC; skips every 3rd attack turn.

## Elemental monsters

### Poison Fly

Both Poison Fly variants apply the established Poison effect with their first attack of the turn. A Green Frog is completely immune to the Poison damage/effect.

- Variant 1 uses **Blunt** physical behavior.
- Variant 2 uses **Axe** physical behavior.

### Firefly

Both Firefly variants use **actual Fire**, not merely light or glow. Their first attack of the turn applies the established Fire effect: d6 duration and 1 Fire damage per turn. A Red Frog is completely immune to the Fire damage/Burning effect.

## Tier scaling

For every tier above Tier 1, each monster receives:

- +1 STR per tier
- +1 DEX per tier
- +1 CON per tier

The existing cumulative ×1.5 enemy-difficulty scaling remains in effect for systems that use the tier multiplier.

Regular enemies still have a 20% chance to carry appropriate loot. Enemy kills themselves grant 0 XP; recovered loot/treasure grants XP according to loot tier.
